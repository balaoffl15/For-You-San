Happiest Birthday, San ❤️🌻

Another year, another reason for me to feel grateful that you’re a part of my life.

When I think about everything we’ve shared, it’s honestly beautiful how naturally you became someone so important to me. There was never a particular moment when it happened. It was built through countless conversations, random talks, laughter, silly moments, difficult days, and all those little memories that became special simply because you were there.

San, one thing I’ll always be grateful for is the way you’ve been there when I needed someone. You listened, understood, cared, and somehow made difficult moments feel a little lighter. There were times when I didn't even know how to explain what I was feeling, yet talking to you still felt easy. I may not have said it enough, but those moments have meant a lot to me.

What I admire most about you is the person you are. Your kindness, your warmth, the way you care about people, and the way you make others feel comfortable just by being yourself. You have this beautiful ability to make even ordinary moments memorable, and I think that's one of the things I'll always cherish about you.

Looking back, it's not just the big moments I remember. It's the random conversations, the stupid laughs, the little jokes, the unexpected talks, and the times you simply showed up when it mattered. Those are the moments that quietly became some of my favourite memories.

Life will keep changing. We'll grow, get busy, enter different phases, and maybe life will take us in directions we can't imagine today. But I hope whatever changes, the memories we've created and the place we hold in each other's lives remain something we can always look back on with a smile.

Thank you for listening.
Thank you for caring.
Thank you for being there.
Thank you for every laugh and every memory.
And most importantly, thank you for being you.

On your birthday, I hope life gives you everything your heart deserves. Happiness that stays, peace that feels effortless, success that makes you proud, people who genuinely value you, and countless moments that make you smile.

I don't know what the next few years will look like, but I know I'll always be grateful that this chapter of my life had you in it.

Here's to more conversations, more laughter, more stupid moments, and many more memories.

Happy Birthday once again, San. ❤️🌻
